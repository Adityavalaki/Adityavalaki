## 01 &nbsp;Featured · Racecraft

A free Windows app that replays every Formula 1 session since 2023 and works out what the timing screens don't show: what each pit stop really cost, how fast each tyre wears, where one driver is quicker than another, and who is likely to win. Race and title odds come from simulating each race thousands of times. The 2026 season was never used for tuning, so it is the honest test.
